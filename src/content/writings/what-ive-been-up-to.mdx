## what have i been building?

### aster

<p className="post-stack">Python · RDKit · scikit-learn · AutoDock Vina · FastAPI · Next.js</p>

in my ongoing progress to learn how ai can help biological research, i researched blind spots that alphafold currently has not considered. one main issue is how alphafold can't tell you what happens when you mutate amino acids in the protein - subsequently making it hard for researchers to figure out what compounds to test next. in my attempt to research the ways ai can automate this decision process, i built an ai decision engine that picks which compounds drug-discovery labs should test next from just a few dozen measurements, and records why each one was picked so it can be checked against the result later

### pokerlab

<p className="post-stack">Swift · SwiftUI · Metal · SwiftData · CloudKit</p>

as an absolute fiend for poker and having been bullied to losing money in cal hacks (jokes), i built a native ios game-theory-optimal poker tutor powered by a custom swift solver engine (hand evaluator, monte carlo equity engine, cfr/dcfr solvers and metal gpu kernels) that computes nash equilibria on device in real time

<p><a className="post-button" href="/projects#pokerlab">see the project →</a></p>

### after you (baymax's cafe) @ la hacks 2026 + build something ai cannot do @ cal

<p className="post-stack">TypeScript · React Three Fiber · Three.js · Web Workers · Vitest</p>

out of spontaneity, i entered in diversatech's "build something ai cannot do" hackathon. with ai coding harnesses being progressively autonomous, it's so easy to fall into the loop of building meaningless slop. so - what is something ai software cannot simply achieve?

that's when i started to explore physical ai and how we can incorporate meaningful, sentient human interactions to our physical robotics. i built a socially-aware robot navigation simulator that replans paths around predicted human movement and conversations via active preference learning + bradley-terry reward model

<p><a className="post-button" href="/projects#after-you">see the project →</a></p>
